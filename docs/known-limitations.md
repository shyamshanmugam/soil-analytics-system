# Known limitations

- No real structured soil-test dataset is included.
- No CNN, gradient-boosted model, SHAP, or Grad-CAM artefact is trained.
- The current score and advice are a transparent baseline, not agronomic validation.
- PostgreSQL persistence, authentication, multilingual UI, Sarvam AI, PDF reports,
  trend forecasting, and deployment infrastructure remain follow-up work.
- Recommendations must be reviewed for local crops, soil conditions, and regulations
  by a qualified agronomist before production use.
